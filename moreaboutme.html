2025 BIPOC Game Conference

Researcher and Panelist

Over the Fall of 2025, I was invited to be a part of the BIPOC Game Conference as a researcher and panelist of my research in indigneous representation in games and how to incorporate culture into game design to 30+ attendees. The conference was a gathering of diverse voices in the gaming industry, focusing on the experiences and contributions of Black, Indigenous, and People of Color in game development. My role was to research games with indigenous representation see how they were protaried and the development process of highlighting their stories in which to provide some idea about incorporating culture into game design was research and cerated during the summer. It was an incredible opportunity to connect with other professionals, share my findings, and contribute to meaningful conversations about diversity and inclusion in gaming.
